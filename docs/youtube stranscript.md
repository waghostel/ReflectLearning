[Intro]
Sometimes we may want to dig into a topic we’re not familiar with. We may ask AI several questions or create a deep research report. Very often, we get lost in scattered information and still only have a vague idea.

ReflectLearning is a tool that helps you learn by starting with one simple question, then gradually stacking more insights as your curiosity grows.

It is an active note-taking app fully created with Google AI Studio. Since it’s built on top of Gemini and Google Search, the knowledge base you build is grounded in real-world evidence. Your notes grow as you ask more questions.

It also helps you reflect on what you’ve learned by testing you with knowledge-point questions.

Let’s explore it by walking through a simple example.

Let’s say you’re interested in understanding the mechanism of antibiotic resistance. You can start by typing your question and using the refine-prompt feature. This helps you quickly draft a simple report structure.

After the search, you’ll receive a first version of the report written in Markdown. It subdivides the knowledge points related to your question into several chapters.

加入第二個新主題
Let’s say you want to explore more fields related to antibiotic resistance. You can use the suggest-topics feature to ask another question closely connected to your main topic.

Now you have two reports generated from two closely related questions. You can use the Reformat feature to merge them into a single report.

During your learning journey, you can always ask more questions or append more reference material if you want to enrich your notebook.


[Learning mode介面介紹]
Just after a few minutes, you now have a report based on the two questions you asked. If you want to dive deeper into any concept, you can ask the AI Tutor. For example, if you want to understand the term bactericidal in more detail, simply ask the AI in the chat.

[Append Paragraph]
We created three features help you further organize your questions into your notes.
The Append Paragraph feature rewrites the question you asked into a short paragraph within the current chapter.

[Append Q&A]
The Append Q&A adds the question and its answer directly into the Q&A section of the current chapter. It also creates a list of questions that link directly to their corresponding answers.

[Create Chapter]
You can also create a new chapter from any question you ask and further expand your knowledge from that key point.

[Rewrite Q&A]
If you find that your Q&A section has become filled with informal questions, you can use the Rewrite Q&A feature to polish and reorganize it.


[Rewrite chapter]
The chapter can also be rewritten using the Rewrite Chapter feature after you have integrated more and more content into it.
