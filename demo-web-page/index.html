Powerful Features for Active Learning

📚

Smart Content Processing

Upload PDFs, documents, images, text, or URLs. AI generates comprehensive, structured learning reports with chapters, Q&A sections, and sources.

💬

Interactive AI Tutor

Chat with an AI tutor that has full context of your learning material. Ask questions, get explanations, and generate visual aids on demand.

🎤

Voice-Based Learning

Use native audio streaming for natural voice conversations with your AI tutor. Learn hands-free with real-time transcription.

🎯

Active Reflection Mode

Switch to an AI coach that proactively asks questions, displays them on a dynamic whiteboard, and guides you through mastery.

🧠

Persistent Memory System

Tracks your understanding per chapter—what you've mastered, what you're struggling with, and your interests for personalized sessions.

✏️

Adaptive Content

Edit chapters, rewrite sections, append new paragraphs from chat responses, and regenerate Q&A sections on demand.

🌐

Web Search Integration

Toggle web search to enrich content with up-to-date information from the internet, ensuring current and comprehensive learning.

🗺️

Structured Navigation

Navigate through chapters with a sidebar, track your progress, and jump to specific sections with ease.

⚡

Real-Time Collaboration

Share learning sessions, collaborate with peers, and get instant feedback. Export reports as PDF or HTML for offline access.
